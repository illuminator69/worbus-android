<p>Worbus is a music app for your own Navidrome server, with one playback session shared across your devices.</p>

<p>Worbus for Android is a fork of Navic by paige (<a href="https://github.com/ssalggnikool/Navic">ssalggnikool/Navic</a>). Licensed GPL-3.0, like the original.</p>

<p>Worbus is not affiliated with Navidrome.</p>
